# Human polish playtest

Start with one complete solo match against Normal AI. A local two-player
session can follow when another player is available. These sessions assess
presentation and enjoyment; Internet validation is a separate backlog item.

Use a development copy whose build identity and completed checks are recorded.
The current default launcher follows `dist/current`; record its resolved path
and package-manifest hash before starting. Do not assume it includes an
unpackaged source change.

## Session

1. Start the game, choose a ruleset and Normal difficulty, and select three
   holdings. Try the controls without coaching first. Note anything unclear.
2. Play a complete match at your normal pace. Use both the battlefield and
   Battle Panel. Let several cinematics finish, then try Skip and inspect how
   the camera returns to your view.
3. When naturally available, watch a crew member deploy and attack, a holding take
   a hit, a Trouble resolve, and a repair. Note objects that clip, float, pop,
   look too similar, or hide the actual target. An action that never occurs
   stays untested.
4. Listen at a comfortable level. Can you distinguish whiskey, supplies,
   abilities and combat? Are repeated sounds tiring or too loud? Try master
   mute, restore your preferred volume, and finish the match.
5. Try a rematch. Report whether the result, next action and difficulty felt
   understandable, and whether you wanted to keep playing.

## Feedback

Use [the session template](playtests/TEMPLATE.md), or send these observations in
chat so they can be recorded without interrupting play:

- The most confusing moment, including the card/action if you remember it.
- The most distracting visual or animation issue.
- A sound that was helpful, missing, repetitive or unpleasant.
- Whether the pace and AI difficulty felt appropriate.
- The one change that would most improve another match.

Record actual observations separately from suggestions. A completed match is
evidence of that session, not proof that all cards, hardware or game modes have
been reviewed. Fix reported issues and repeat the affected actions before
closing them.
