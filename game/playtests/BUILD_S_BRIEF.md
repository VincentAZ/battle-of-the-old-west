# Build S playtest brief

Status: prepared; human session not performed.

This Linux development build is for reviewing game polish. It includes the
latest knee/elbow movement, Vigilante's fitted chest patch, and the earlier
hair, portrait, sound and camera changes. The art is still being refined.

Start with the launcher inside the Build S package. The project's usual
launcher still opens Build P. Before playing, read that package's
`REVIEW_NOTES.md` for its completed checks and known issues.

Play one complete solo match using Boomtown and Normal AI, at a pace and
volume that feel comfortable. Try the controls without coaching first. Watch
several cinematics finish, then try Skip. Notice whether the card action and
target are clear, and whether the camera returns where you expect.

When the match naturally presents them, watch a deployment, attack, person
fall, camp damage and repair. Listen for water, supplies and repeated combat
sounds. Try master mute once, then restore your preferred volume. Actions
that never occur remain untested; there is no need to force a particular card.

After the match, send these observations in chat or fill in `TEMPLATE.md`:

- What was the most confusing moment?
- Which visual or movement distracted you most?
- Which sound was useful, tiring, too loud or missing?
- How did the pace and AI difficulty feel?
- Would you play another match, and what one change would help most?

A local two-player session can follow when another player is available. Record
only what was actually played and heard.
