## Character and environment assets

The human body, facial shapes and skin originate from **Vitruvian**, by Sean
Buckley and Olaf Delgado-Friedrichs, with contributors recorded in the
[CharMorph character configuration](https://github.com/Upliner/CharMorph-Vitruvian/blob/main/config.yaml).
Hope contributed the shirt and pants. These source character assets are CC0.
The prepared human meshes, eyes and hair came through
[Agile Lens / VitruvianGodot](https://github.com/ibrews/VitruvianGodot).
The original source notices, licenses and pinned download checksums are
preserved in the project's `art/characters/vitruvian` directory.

Photographic surfaces, skies and scanned rocks are from **Poly Haven**, CC0:

- Rusty Metal Sheet — Amal Kumar.
- Concrete Wall 007 — Dario Barresi, Rico Cilliers and Charlotte Baglioni.
- Dirt Aerial 03 — Rob Tuytel; Dirt — Charlotte Baglioni.
- Rust Coarse 01 — Dimitrios Savva and Rico Cilliers.
- Rock 07 — Jenelle van Heerden; Coast Rocks 05 — Rob Tuytel.
- Kloppenheim 06 Pure Sky and Qwantani Sunset Pure Sky — Greg Zaal and Jarod Guest.
- Fabric Pattern 05, Fabric Pattern 07 and Leather Red 02 — creator metadata
  preserved with the project's source downloads.

Asset pages and exact download provenance are listed in the source project's
art library and character source folders. The holding structures, costume
additions and depot assembly were created for this project. The audio is original procedural synthesis.

## Unreal Engine assets

Unreal Engine, the template animations and the rifle model are supplied by
Epic Games. These assets are separate from the CC0 character and environment
sources above and are not described or relicensed here as CC0. The source
project retains its engine and asset provenance separately.

The fire and smoke flipbook textures are bundled Epic assets from Unreal's
NetworkPredictionExtras content. The project retains their original hashes and
source paths in `art/effects/fire-study/texture-export.json`; these textures are
also separate from the CC0 sources. The layered fire material and component
were authored for this project.
